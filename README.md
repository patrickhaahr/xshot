# XShot

XShot is a Chromium browser extension for turning X posts and page elements into one tightly cropped, full-height image—even when they extend beyond the screen.

It makes sharing a Post with its Conversation easy, without manually cropping images or stitching captures together. XShot captures the page as your browser renders it, preserving its theme, media and current video or GIF frame, without using the X API.

## Features

- **Post Capture on X:** right-click a Post to capture it and the Conversation leading up to it, from the root Post through the chosen Post. Replies below it are excluded.
- **Expanded text and less clutter:** Post Captures expand “Show more” text and remove interface controls such as menus, follow buttons and the reply composer.
- **Pick mode on any site:** click the toolbar button, hover to highlight an element, and use the mouse wheel or ↑/↓ to widen or narrow the Target. Escape cancels.
- **Clipboard or PNG download:** copy by default, use “Screenshot post to file” on X, or Shift-click in Pick mode to save a PNG.
- **Sharp, full-height images:** capture beyond the viewport at 2× resolution, in a single image.
- **Clear feedback:** see a thumbnail after delivery, warnings when content could not load or expand, and a refusal when a Target is too tall to capture at full sharpness.
